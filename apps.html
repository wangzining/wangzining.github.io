Pokémon GO PvP Explorer

An interactive data visualization tool for Pokémon GO PvP competitive rankings, powered by pvpoke.com simulation data.

  • Stat Explorer — scatter plot of Great / Ultra / Master League rankings. Pick any two axes (Score, Atk, Def, HP, Stat Product) and click a dot to see full stats, best moveset, matchups & counters.
  • Moveset Explorer — search by Pokémon name to see all its moves with simulation-usage bars, or search by move name (e.g. "shadow claw") to find every Pokémon in the meta that has it.
